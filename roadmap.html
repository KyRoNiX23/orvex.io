Future direction

Roadmap

Ideas for growing Orvex into a richer sandbox. These are proposals, not committed delivery dates.

Ideas to explore

Priorities can change as technical constraints and community feedback become clearer.

01 · World diversity

Expand biome variety, terrain features, vegetation and points of interest.

02 · Mod tools & API

Document stable extension points and provide tools for creating and testing mods.

03 · Creative tools

Improve building workflows, world editing and content iteration.

04 · Performance

Profile chunk generation, rendering and memory use; prioritize measurable optimizations.

05 · Multiplayer research

Investigate networking architecture and synchronization requirements.

06 · Community hub

Consider a place to share creations, documentation and community-made content.

07 · World events

Explore quests, discoveries and optional world events once core gameplay supports them.

08 · Accessibility & UX

Improve controls, settings, onboarding and interface customization.

Have an idea? Open a GitHub issue or discussion in the repository. Roadmap items may be revised or removed.
